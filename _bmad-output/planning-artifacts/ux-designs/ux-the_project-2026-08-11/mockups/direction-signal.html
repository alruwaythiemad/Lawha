Direction D — “Signal”

Minimal as reduction to zero. No radii, no shadows, no gradients, no tints. Structure is 1px black rules; the only colour in the product is a status block, and it is always solid — never a soft background wash.

This direction resolves your tension most bluntly: an offline screen produces a full-bleed red strip across the page. It is genuinely impossible to miss. It is also loud in a way that could read as alarming for a single screen having a two-minute Wi-Fi wobble.

Highest-contrast and the strongest accessibility floor by default. Carries the most brand personality and therefore the most risk — this is a look people either love or find shouty, and it constrains the marketing site to match.
