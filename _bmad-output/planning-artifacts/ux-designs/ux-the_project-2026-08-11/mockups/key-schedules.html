What this screen refuses to do. Group E defines four precedence rules — screen beats branch, narrower beats wider, later beats earlier, fallback plays last. This surface never shows them, never explains them, and never asks the owner to apply them. It shows the answer: what plays, and when. Where a window he created is being overridden, it says which one wins in one sentence at the moment he's looking at it.

Note the second consequence, from DESIGN.md: because colour is reserved for signal, playlists cannot be colour-coded. Blocks are distinguished by fill inversion and label. The only coloured block here is amber — the hours where nothing is scheduled and the wall falls back to a holding card.
