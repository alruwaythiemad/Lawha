Direction C — “Room”

Minimal but warm. A paper ground with white cards floating on it, soft radii, sage instead of green, terracotta instead of emergency red. Ink is a warm near-black, never pure #000.

Escalation happens by the card changing its ground colour and growing — it gains a plain sentence and a button, so the owner is never told there's a problem without being told what to do (NFR-9).

Lowest technical register of the four. Fits a non-technical café owner and reads well on a phone; risks looking soft to a customer managing forty screens, and the warm palette narrows your accent options later.
