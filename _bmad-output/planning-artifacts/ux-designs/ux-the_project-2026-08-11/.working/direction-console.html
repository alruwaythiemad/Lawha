Direction B — “Console”

Minimal as precision instrument. Dense 40px rows, 13px type, tabular numerals, visible 1px structure — the aesthetic of Linear and Vercel rather than of print. Nothing decorative, but nothing hidden either.

Calm-until-it-matters is carried by a 6px status dot and an escalation that expands the row inline into a full sentence. Degraded rows also tint their background, and a page-level banner appears above the table.

Best fit for the multi-branch owner scanning forty screens. The single-screen café owner may find it reads as software for someone more technical than them.
