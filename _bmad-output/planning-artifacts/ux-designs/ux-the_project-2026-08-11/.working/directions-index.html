Four readings of “minimalist + modern”

Same hero screen — the console Screens list — rendered four ways. Each file also shows the Arabic RTL mirror, the empty state, and both player registers.

These are not palette swaps. They differ on density, type weight, brand register, and how loudly a broken screen announces itself. Pick one, or tell me which parts of which.

A — Wall

editorial · low density · no motion

Print discipline. Hairline rules instead of cards, large display type, and one colour reserved entirely for alarm — healthy screens carry no badge and no green at all.

B — Console

precision instrument · dense · 120ms

Linear/Vercel register. 40px rows, 13px type, tabular numerals, visible 1px structure. Status is a 6px dot; escalation expands the row inline and raises a page banner.

C — Room

warm · medium density · 240ms

Paper ground, white cards, soft radii. Sage instead of green, terracotta instead of emergency red. Escalation grows the card and hands over a button.

D — Signal

high contrast · flat · zero motion

Reduction to zero. No radii, no shadows, no tints. 1px black rules and solid colour blocks. An offline screen produces a full-bleed red strip across the page.
