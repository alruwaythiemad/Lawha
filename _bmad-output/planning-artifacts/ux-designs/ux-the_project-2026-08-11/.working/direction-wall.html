Direction A — “Wall”

Signage is a print discipline before it is a software one, so this direction borrows from print: hairline rules instead of cards, no shadows, no fills, and one accent colour reserved entirely for alarm. Healthy screens carry no badge, no dot, no green — the absence of colour is the healthy state.

That makes the escalation impossible to miss, because it's the only coloured thing on the page. The cost is that a first-time owner has to learn that quiet means good.

Low density, large display type, generous air. Reads confident and editorial; risks reading austere.
